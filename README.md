# Ontology for Research Data Access (ORDA)

ORDA models the research access application process under the European Union's Digital Services Act Article 40. Article 40 has two access provisions, one for public and one for pnon public data. While ORDA models both, there are further processes such as mediation, appeal that are not yet modeled.

## Legislation

- [Digital Services Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065) — Regulation (EU) 2022/2065. Article 40 grants researchers access to platform data to investigate the systemic risks identified under Article 34.
- [Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2050](http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2025/2050/oj) — the delegated act on data access, setting out the Article 40(4) procedure: the DSA Data Access Portal, the assessment by the Digital Services Coordinator, and the content of the reasoned request.

To model data requested, ORDA links to ActivityStreams2.0 rather than defining its own classes for social media data. As data providers publish their data catalogues in greater detail ORDA will be expanded to cover specific fields mentioned in data providers' catalogues. 

ORDA is a draft under review and should not be considered a specification.
ORDA is a project of DSA40 Collaboratory and the University of Oxford.
