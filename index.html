Abstract

ORDA models the process by which vetted researchers obtain access to platform data under Article 40 of the EU Digital Services Act (Regulation 2022/2065), for the purpose of studying systemic risks. It covers the actors involved, the request lifecycle, the data made available, and the safeguards attached to it.

The ontology's organising distinction is between the two access routes that Article 40 creates: the Art. 40(4) route, in which a request for non-public data is mediated by a Digital Services Coordinator and results in a reasoned request to the platform, and the Art. 40(12) route, in which publicly accessible data is requested directly from the platform without intermediation. These are modelled as distinct classes because they differ in who may apply, who assesses the application, and what the platform is obliged to provide.

3. Introduction

Article 40 of the Digital Services Act obliges providers of Very Large Online Platforms (VLOPs) and Very Large Online Search Engines (VLOSEs) to give researchers access to data for studying systemic risks in the Union. Delegated Regulation 2025/2050 sets out the procedure for the non-public data route. The process spans several actors — researchers and their institutions, the Digital Services Coordinator of establishment, the coordinator of the researcher's own member state, and the platform — and moves through a sequence of assessments with defined outcomes.

ORDA gives that process a machine-readable vocabulary. It types the requests, the parties, the assessments and their statuses, the data catalogues platforms must publish, and the technical, legal and organisational safeguards attached to an access modality. It reuses established vocabularies wherever one already covers the ground — 13 of them, listed in section 12 — and defines new terms only for what is specific to the Article 40 regime.

13. Example Data

Illustrative instance data is distributed separately, in orda-examples.ttl: a worked Art. 40(4) application from a university researcher to a designated platform, with its status history, data management plan, and the CSV, JSON and PDF distributions returned.
